  3. Placement khong khai bao default -> tat (enable=false). Gia tri toan cuc cuoi cung: total_timeout 15000, timeout_ms 10000, refresh 0/0, high_floor tat, gone_in_test_mode=false.
  4. Muon TAT placement tu console: dat "enable": false (khong dung "" hay xoa key).
[CHOT owner 2026-11-23] Phan tu tiers.normal: truong "enable" thieu -> mac dinh TRUE (khac lib cu enable_ad=false). Placement khong co default nhung JSON co enable:true -> bat, truong thieu lay gia tri toan cuc.

6.2 FullScreenGate
- Trang thai Idle | Reserved(owner) | Showing(owner). Reserved gom: man cho rewarded, showThen dang cho ad, lop phu App Open dang cho tai.
- WATCHDOG (H5): sau show(), neu khong co dismissed/failed callback: nha co khi mot Activity khong phai ad cua app ON_RESUME, hoac khi qua tran thoi gian (de xuat 2 phut). Phat event.

6.3 High floor

- Lib TU BO high floor khi co nguoi cho (waiter) hoac khi format la Native / AppOpen-reactive (khong phu thuoc quy uoc config) [DE XUAT]. Chi bat cho preload nen.
- Ket qua den sau cap: request SDK van bay, luot moi da bat dau -> co the thua 1 ad cung khoa; chap nhan, ghi nhan va test.
6.4 InterCap / App Open [DE XUAT - CAN OWNER, xem Q-A]
